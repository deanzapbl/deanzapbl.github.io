About PBL

The Group
For Growth.

At De Anza College Phi Beta Lambda, our mission is to create a constructively critical environment that facilitates exploration, ethical decision-making, and professional development for all De Anza students. We are united by a commitment to excellence, professionalism, and growth — empowering aspiring business leaders through professional development, innovation, and collaboration. Our chapter provides an inclusive and dynamic environment where students develop real-world skills, lead with purpose, and drive positive impact in their communities and future industries.

We lead with Inclusion, act with Integrity, stay driven by Curiosity, embrace Courage, and win through Collaboration.
