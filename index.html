01 — About

The Group
for Growth.

Phi Beta Lambda at De Anza College is the premier student business organization cultivating the next generation of industry professionals. Founded on the principles of leadership, ambition, and inclusivity, PBL provides every student — regardless of background — access to real-world professional opportunities.

Our community is a resource conglomerate for all De Anza students: a place where cohorts convene, careers begin, and professional identity is forged. We set the standard for extracurricular excellence on campus.

03 — Recruitment

Join the
Chapter.

  1. 01
    Application Opens
    Submit your interest form during the open application window each quarter. Open to all De Anza students — no prior business experience required.
  2. 02
    Zoom Interviews
    Selected applicants are invited for a 15-minute virtual interview with members of the Executive Board. Monday through Wednesday following application close.
  3. 03
    Decisions Released
    Offers are sent to successful candidates. Signed contracts are due within 48 hours of receiving an offer. Welcome to the chapter.
  4. 04
    IM Orientation
    New internal members attend a mandatory orientation to align with PBL's mission, standards, cohorts, and the academic calendar ahead.

Ready to grow?

Applications open each quarter. Follow @deanzapbl on Instagram for recruitment announcements, interview timelines, and orientation dates.

05 — Polaris Research Group

Guided by
the North Star.

Polaris Research Group is the research body of Phi Beta Lambda — the North Star of our organization. Named for the guiding star of Ursa Minor, Polaris exists within PBL as a specialized research cohort producing rigorous, publication-ready equity and macro research.

Polaris members publish sector analyses, equity models, and strategic research under the PBL umbrella — bridging the gap between student finance and institutional-quality work, with publications targeting SSRN and academic venues.
