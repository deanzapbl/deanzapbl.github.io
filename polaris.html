About Polaris

Polaris Research Group (PRG) is a selective cohort within Phi Beta Lambda designed to cultivate elite undergraduate talent through rigorous, high-impact research and strategic thought leadership.

Inspired by the North Star, Polaris represents our commitment to guiding students into the premier worlds of high finance, strategy, technology, and macroeconomics. Through sector-based research, executive mentorship, and thought-driven publications, PRG is where leadership meets specialization.
