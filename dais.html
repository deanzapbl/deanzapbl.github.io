About DAIS 2026

Find Your
Tempo.

Find Your Tempo is about finding your place and your footing in your career. No two paths move at the same pace. This year, our speakers and judges share how they found theirs, and what that journey can look like for community college students starting right here.

Why DAIS

Community college is where a lot of careers quietly begin. DAIS puts a real business challenge, real industry judges, and people who have walked this road in one room, so finding your footing does not have to wait until after transfer.

What We Aim To Do

  • Find your footingTake on a real brand's challenge and see what your skills can do.
  • Learn from their pathHear how industry professionals found their tempo, and what they would do in your shoes.
  • Build your momentumLeave with feedback, connections, and a clearer next step in your career.

The Prompt

Apple Music.

You are: Apple Music's strategy teamTarget: U.S. ages 18–24Rivals: Spotify, YouTube Music

Apple Music operates in one of the world's largest and most competitive digital entertainment markets. In the United States, Spotify and YouTube Music compete heavily for younger listeners, particularly Gen Z consumers ages 18–24, who place strong value on personalization, discovery, social sharing, and cultural relevance.

You are part of Apple Music's strategy team. Apple leadership wants to strengthen Apple Music's position among U.S. consumers ages 18–24 and make the service a more compelling choice for both existing Apple users and listeners currently using competing platforms.

Develop a strategy that helps Apple Music grow its market position among U.S. Gen Z and young adult listeners ages 18–24 and differentiate itself from competitors such as Spotify and YouTube Music.

Your recommendation should go beyond simply lowering prices or copying an existing competitor feature.

Consider how Apple Music could use its ecosystem, partnerships, technology, brand, content, pricing, marketing, or customer experience to attract new users and increase loyalty among existing subscribers.

EcosystemPartnershipsTechnologyBrandContentPricingMarketingCustomer Experience
1Define the Competitive ProblemIdentify the biggest challenge preventing Apple Music from gaining ground among U.S. consumers ages 18–24. What gives competitors an advantage today?
2Proposed StrategyDevelop a specific strategy Apple Music could use to strengthen its position within this market.
3Competitive AdvantageExplain why your strategy would work and what makes it difficult for competitors to replicate.
4Implementation & Success MetricsExplain how Apple Music should execute your strategy and identify the key metrics you would use to determine whether it is successful.
